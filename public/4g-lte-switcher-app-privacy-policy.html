Privacy Policy

4G LTE Switcher App · com.kliksob.forgswitch2

A clear, human-friendly summary of what we collect, why we collect it, and how you stay in control.

4G LTE Switcher App takes your privacy seriously. This document explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have — in plain English.

1   Routine Information Collection

Like most web servers, we log basic information about visitors — including IP address, browser type, timestamp, and referring page. None of this can personally identify a specific visitor. It is tracked solely for routine administration and maintenance.

2   Cookies & Web Beacons

When needed, 4G LTE Switcher App uses cookies to store visitor preferences and history to better serve you and/or present tailored content.

Third-party advertisers and partners may also use cookies, scripts, and/or web beacons to track visitors to our app/website to display ads and other useful information. Such tracking is done directly by the third party through their own servers and is subject to their own privacy policy.

3   Controlling Your Privacy

You can change your browser settings to disable cookies at any time if you have privacy concerns. Disabling cookies for all sites is not recommended as it may interfere with some services. The best approach is to manage cookies on a per-site basis. Consult your browser documentation for instructions on how to block cookies and other tracking mechanisms.

Tip

Use Incognito or Android’s Clear data to test cookie behavior without wiping your main preferences.

4   A Special Note About Google Advertising

Any ads served by Google, Inc. and affiliated companies may be controlled using cookies. These cookies allow Google to display ads based on your visits to this app/website and other sites that use Google’s ad services. Learn how to opt out of Google’s cookie usage. As mentioned above, any tracking by Google through cookies and other mechanisms is subject to Google’s own privacy policy.

5   Contact Information

If you have questions about the privacy policy for 4G LTE Switcher App, you can contact us via email at sksnet.id@gmail.com — we usually reply within 24 hours.
